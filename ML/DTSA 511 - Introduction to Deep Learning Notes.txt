Describe the significance of 'width' in neural networks.
- It affects the network's capacity to model complex functions
- it determines the number of neurons in each layer.

Best descriptions of the concept of 'backpropagation' in neural networks??
- Backward of propagation of errors to update weights.

A limitation of using linear activations in neural networks?
- They cannot model complex patterns.

The 'depth' of a neural network refers to?
- the number of layers in the network

The primary purpose of an activation function in a neural network?
- to introduce non-linearity to the network.

These statements correctly describes the mathematical representation of neural network layers?
- Activation functions are applied element-wise after matrix multiplication and bias addition in each layer

Training and Regularizing Neural Networks Quiz

Patterns in training curves indicate overfitting
- Large and growing gap between training and validation loss
- Validation loss starts increasing while training loss continues decreasing
- Training accuracy is much higher than validation accuracy.

If your neural network shows both training and validation loss remaining high throughout training, what is the most likely diagnosis?
- Underfitting

What is the main mechanism by which dropouts prevents overfitting?
- Randomly setting neuron activations to zero forces the network to learn robust, distributed representations.

What does Adam maintain in addition to momentum's first moment estimate?
- A second moment estimate that tracks the squared gradients for per-parameter learning rate adaption.

The core idea behind L2 regularization (weight decay)?
-Penalize large weights by adding Lambda Sigma w^2 to the loss function.

The one epoch in the context of neural network training?
- One complete pass through all training data.

When monitoring training, why is it important to track both training loss and validation loss?
- The gap between them reveals whether the model is overfitting.

Where is the bath normalization typically placed in a neural network layer?
- After the linear transformation and before the activation function.

What is a characteristic symptom of a learning rate that is too large?
- The loss oscillates or diverges to infinity/NaN

Why neural networks with many parameters tend to overfit?
-They can memorize the training data including nose.
