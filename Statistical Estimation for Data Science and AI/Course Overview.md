## Exam topic map
1. Start Here! — policies, syllabus, AI citation policy, Jupyter lab setup
2. Point Estimation — recognizing discrete distributions, calculations with continuous distributions, probability/expectation/variance review, good estimators (unbiasedness, variance, MSE), method of moments
3. Maximum Likelihood Estimation — likelihood functions, finding MLEs for one- and two-parameter models, invariance property, mean-squared error and efficiency
4. Large Sample Properties of MLEs — asymptotic unbiasedness, asymptotic normality, Cramér–Rao lower bound, further computations with MLEs
5. Confidence Intervals Involving the Normal Distribution — interval estimation, correct CI interpretation, CIs for a mean (large/small samples, variance known vs. unknown), CIs for differences between means
6. Beyond Normality: Confidence Intervals Unleashed! — CIs for proportions and variances, two-sample CIs, building CIs for parameters of non-normal distributions
