## Exam topic map
1. Start Here! — policies, syllabus, AI citation policy, Jupyter/R setup
2. Descriptive Statistics and the Axioms of Probability — sample spaces, axioms, inclusion–exclusion, counting (permutations, combinations)
3. Conditional Probability — conditional probability, multiplication rule, law of total probability, Bayes' theorem, independent events
4. Discrete Random Variables — pmf/cdf, Bernoulli, geometric, binomial, negative binomial, expectation and variance
5. Continuous Random Variables — pdf/cdf, uniform, exponential, normal (standardizing, Φ table), Poisson & exponential, normal approximation to binomial
6. Joint Distributions and Covariance — joint/marginal pmfs, E[g(X,Y)], covariance, correlation, variance of sums
7. The Central Limit Theorem — sums/linear combinations of normals, CLT for X̄ and sums, continuity correction
