#### Part (c) 

Let $p = 0.5$. Find $P(X = 12)$. Round your answer to four decimal places. Save your answer as `p2.3`.

[CODE]
p2.3 = NA
p <- 0.5
x <- 12
wins_needed <- 9

# A needs 8 wins in the first 11 games (binomial), then wins game 12 (factor p);
# B is the mirror image with p and 1 - p swapped.
p_A <- p       * dbinom(wins_needed - 1, x - 1, p)
p_B <- (1 - p) * dbinom(wins_needed - 1, x - 1, 1 - p)
p2.3 <- round(p_A + p_B, 4)   # 0.0806
