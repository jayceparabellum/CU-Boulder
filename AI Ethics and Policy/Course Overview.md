## How grading works
- **Lab notebooks:** peer-reviewed by 3 classmates, graded on completion and coherence. Fill them in as you go through each module, export to PDF, and submit at the end of the module. **For-credit learners: peer reviews are due 3 days before the session ends.**
- **Quizzes:** one per module (two in Module 1). They cover both the readings and the lectures. Multiple attempts are usually allowed.

## AI-use rules for this course (from "Assessment Expectations")
AI use is **conditional** for quizzes, peer reviews, and the final project.

**Allowed:** summarizing readings you've read · practice questions · brainstorming and outlines · tutoring or feedback on your own drafts · spelling and grammar · editing text you wrote yourself (without adding new ideas).

**Not allowed:** entering assignment or quiz questions (exact or paraphrased) into an AI tool · AI-generated answers in place of your own thinking · answers to prompts that closely resemble graded ones · any AI help on a graded item that you don't document.

**You must document AI use.** Add an acknowledgement section to each submission that lists the tool, the purpose, your prompts, and how you changed the output. Keep a record of the session as well. A statement you can adapt for these study materials:
